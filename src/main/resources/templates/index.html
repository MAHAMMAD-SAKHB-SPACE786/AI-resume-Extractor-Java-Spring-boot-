How it works

    
Five steps, all running on your own computer.

    

      
1
Read

        
Apache PDFBox reads your PDF in memory. Nothing is written to disk or a database.

      
2
Understand

        
Rules split your resume into sections. A local Llama 3.2 model finds skills in your experience and projects.

      
3
Match

        
Each role skill is labelled proven, listed, AI-inferred or missing. Embeddings spot related skills you already know.

      
4
Score

        
55% skills, 20% evidence, 15% impact and 10% structure, so a skill with proof counts more than a skill in a list.

      
5
Guide

        
RAG searches a vector database for the best learning resource per gap. The AI coach rewrites weak bullet points.
